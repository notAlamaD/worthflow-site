Everything you own, in one number

WorthFlow tracks your net worth across accounts, currencies and assets — on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Private by construction: your data stays on your devices.

What it does

  • Accounts in any currency, converted to a single base currency you choose.
  • Cash, deposits, crypto, and anything else you want to count as capital.
  • History and charts, so you can see the direction rather than just today's balance.
  • Optional read-only connections to Monobank and Binance, plus statement file import.
  • Optional iCloud sync between your own devices.
  • Face ID lock, and an export file whenever you want your data out.
  • English, Russian, Ukrainian and Bulgarian.

Privacy

No account to create, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs. The developer receives no data from the app at all. Bank tokens live in the device Keychain and never leave the device. The full details are in the privacy policy.
