Common questions

Do I have to connect a bank?

No. The app works fully without any connection — you can add accounts and update balances by hand, and import statement files. Connections just save you the typing.

Is a connection able to move my money?

No. Connections are read-only: WorthFlow reads balances and history and nothing else. It cannot make payments or trades, and it never asks for your banking password — only a token that you issue yourself and can revoke at any time in your bank or exchange account.

Where is my data kept?

On your device. If you turn on iCloud Sync, a copy also lives in your own private iCloud so your other devices see the same portfolio. Nothing is sent to the developer. See the privacy policy for the details.

iCloud sync doesn't seem to start

Two things to check. First, you need to be signed in to iCloud in system Settings. Second, the setting takes effect after you restart the app — the sync container is set up once when the app launches. Settings → Sync shows the current status.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Either enable iCloud Sync on both devices, or use Settings → Export to write a backup file and import it on the new device. Note that bank tokens are deliberately excluded from backups — you re-add the connection once on the new device.

Why do the totals differ slightly from my bank app?

Anything held in another currency is converted at the latest rate WorthFlow fetched, which will not match your bank's rate at the exact same second. Rates are informational.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app. If iCloud Sync was on, remove the synced copy in iOS Settings → your Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

WorthFlow is not a bank, a broker, or a source of investment advice. Exchange rates and prices are shown for information only.
