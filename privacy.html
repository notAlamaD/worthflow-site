Privacy Policy

WorthFlow does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, and no tracking.

Last updated: 26 August 2026

WorthFlow is a personal capital tracker for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It records what you own and what it is worth over time. This policy explains exactly what the app does with that information.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer of WorthFlow receives no data from the app — no personal information, no financial information, no usage statistics, no crash reports, no device or advertising identifiers.

There is no sign-up and no user account. The app contains no analytics framework, no advertising framework, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter — accounts, balances, assets, transactions and history — is stored locally on your device, inside the app's private storage, protected by the device's own file encryption.

If you turn on iCloud Sync in Settings, that same data is also stored in your own private iCloud database so it can appear on your other devices. It is held in your personal iCloud account under Apple's terms; the developer of WorthFlow has no access to it and cannot read it. Turning the setting off stops syncing.

Backup files you create with the export feature are written wherever you choose to save them. They are plain files under your control, and the app does not upload them anywhere.

Network connections the app makes

The app talks to a small, fixed set of services, and only for the purposes below. None of them receive your balances, your holdings, or anything that identifies you.

ServicePurposeWhat is sent
api.frankfurter.dev Currency exchange rates Currency codes only (e.g. USD, EUR)
api.coingecko.com Cryptocurrency prices Asset identifiers only (e.g. bitcoin)
api.monobank.ua Optional bank connection Your personal Monobank token, only if you add one
api.binance.com Optional exchange connection Your Binance API key signature, only if you add one

All requests use HTTPS. The rate services are contacted regardless of whether you connect a bank; the bank and exchange services are contacted only if you set up that connection yourself.

Bank and exchange connections

Connections are entirely optional — the app is fully functional without them, and you can enter balances by hand.

  • Connections are read-only. WorthFlow reads balances and transaction history. It cannot move money and never initiates payments or trades.
  • You supply your own token or API key, issued by you in your bank or exchange account. The app never asks for your banking password or login credentials.
  • Tokens and API secrets are stored in the iOS Keychain with the ThisDeviceOnly protection level. They stay on the one device: they are not synced to iCloud Keychain, are not included in device backups, and are never transmitted anywhere except to the service they belong to.
  • Removing a connection in the app deletes its stored secret from the Keychain.

Face ID and Touch ID

If you enable the app lock, authentication is performed by iOS. WorthFlow is told only whether authentication succeeded. Your biometric data is never available to the app.

Sharing and selling

Your data is never sold, rented, shared, or used for advertising or profiling. There is no mechanism in the app by which this could happen.

Children

WorthFlow is a general-audience finance tool and is not directed at children. Since no data is collected, none is collected from children either.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its local data from that device. If iCloud Sync was enabled, the synced copy can be removed in iOS Settings under your Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Backup files you exported yourself are deleted like any other file.
